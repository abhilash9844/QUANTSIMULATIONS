# Quantitative Simulations

A collection of quantitative finance simulations, discrete stochastic processes, and probability experiments in Python.

## Projects & Scripts

- **[Random Walk Probability Evolution](random-walk-probability-evolution/README.md)**: Monte Carlo verification of the discrete one-step probability evolution equation for a symmetric random walk, complete with trajectory visualization, exact binomial comparison, and empirical transition probability checks.
- `DicePsum7.py`: Dice roll sum probability simulation.
- `RadonNikodym.py`: Exploration of change of measure and Radon–Nikodym derivatives in discrete probability spaces.
- `basicbuysell.py`: Basic simulation of buy and sell market execution logic.
